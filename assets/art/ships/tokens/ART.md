# Ship art provenance

These original Arkflight designs were generated with the built-in image-generation tool during the October 2026 ship-token design session. User-supplied fantasy ship images informed the miniature art finish. Museum ship models, plans and surviving hulls informed the five historical adaptations. These are fantasy designs, not exact historical reconstructions. Reference photographs and drawings are not bundled with the module.

The conversion step encodes the selected generated PNGs as WebP at quality 70 using Pillow/libwebp, method 6, with exact transparent-pixel handling. It preserves the original canvas size and verifies the decoded alpha channel byte-for-byte against the source. No new image generation, cropping, resizing, background removal, recoloring or retouching occurs during this conversion. Generated glow and edge pixels are retained as supplied.
