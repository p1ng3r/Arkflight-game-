# Arkflight Ship Talents — Whole-Ship Design Principle

**Status:** Current design lock for ship progression.  
**Branch:** `dev`

This document supersedes any earlier ship-talent design that asks the player to spend a Ship Talent Point to improve only one officer station.

## Core rule

A Ship Talent represents growth of the vessel and crew as a single operating whole.

A player should not have to choose **Captain over Navigator**, **Engineer over Veilwarden**, or any other station merely to receive a basic permanent ship-progression bonus.

Station identity still matters during play because each station has different actions, officer abilities, Arkcraft, responsibilities, and checks. Ship Talents should improve the vessel's overall doctrine, systems, coordination, or capabilities rather than declare one station mechanically more important than another.

## Foundation training replacement

The following five talents are retired and no longer appear in the selectable Ship Talent catalog:

- Captain's Vessel
- Engineer's Vessel
- Navigator's Vessel
- Battlewatch Vessel
- Veilwarden's Vessel

They are replaced by two whole-ship Foundation talents, each costing 1 TP:

### Voyage-Trained Crew — 1 TP

Gain **+1 to all station rolls during Voyage Events**.

### Battle-Trained Crew — 1 TP

Gain **+1 to all station rolls during Ship Combat**.

This bonus applies to station checks made during combat. It does **not** add another permanent modifier to direct ship weapon attack rolls; ship weapon attacks continue to use the dedicated Battlewatch/ship/weapon attack formula.

A Level 1 ship begins with 2 TP and may therefore purchase either training plus another Foundation talent, or purchase both and become broadly trained in Voyage and Combat immediately.

Saving TP remains legal, but early progression must not require saving merely because meaningful choices are unavailable.

## Legacy vessels

Existing vessels that still contain one or more of the five retired talent IDs receive no benefit from those retired IDs and those IDs consume no TP. This functions as an automatic refund until the vessel's talent build is next edited/resaved.

## Ongoing design requirement

When the remaining 20-level talent catalog is revised, station-specific talents should be reviewed against this rule. A talent may interact with a station as part of its execution, but the **ship-level purchase should represent a meaningful gain to the vessel as a whole**, not a permanent choice that one officer station matters more than another.
