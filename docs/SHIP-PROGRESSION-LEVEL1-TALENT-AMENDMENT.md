# Arkflight Ship Progression Amendment — Level 1 Talent

**Status:** Locked amendment to `docs/SHIP-PROGRESSION-TALENTS-MODS.md`

This amendment supersedes the prior Talent Point economy language that stated a level 1 ship begins with 0 earned Talent Points.

## Locked Rule

A level 1 Arkflight ship begins with **1 Talent Point** and may purchase one Foundation talent immediately.

Each later ship level grants **1 additional Talent Point**.

| Ship Level | Total Earned Talent Points |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 5 | 5 |
| 10 | 10 |
| 15 | 15 |
| 20 | 20 |

The level-1 Talent Point represents the first deliberate customization of an otherwise off-the-lot vessel. Ship level itself still grants no automatic increase to Hull, Lifeveil, Morale, Speed, Facing, Strain Limit, weapon capacity, or any other base statistic.

A player may spend the level-1 point on any Foundation talent for which the ship qualifies.

Milestone Calling/Signature/Legendary/Mythic abilities at levels 5, 10, 15, and 20 are separate progression rewards and do not consume the normal Talent Point for that level unless later balance testing explicitly changes that rule.

All other progression rules in `SHIP-PROGRESSION-TALENTS-MODS.md` remain unchanged unless separately amended.
