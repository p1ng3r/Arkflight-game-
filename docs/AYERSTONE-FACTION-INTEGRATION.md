## Harbor law and faction identity

Source: [Factions of Known Space](https://github.com/p1ng3r/Ayerstone-Setting-Guide/blob/1f1259272eeb910b532ed8415c139645e07a2f67/docs/factions/index.md).

- Ayerstone is an independent, Freespacer-aligned neutral port, governed locally rather than by an outside empire.
- The Open Dock Compact permits vessels to request harbor, aid, repairs, trade, and refuge while obeying city law.
- Foreign wars and foreign enforcement cannot override harbor jurisdiction. Slaving, abduction, forced conscription, and unauthorized seizure of persons are prohibited within city law.
- Entry to harbor does not imply trust or freedom from scrutiny. Even Brotherhood and Grelkin ships can request docking under the same law.
- Freespacer identity, Freebooter service, a Great House contract, employment, and family membership describe different relationships. They should not be collapsed into one exclusive faction choice.

**Proposed gameplay consequence:** Separate docking eligibility, lawful conduct, professional access, faction relationships, and obligations. A favorable relationship should not automatically override harbor law, and an unfriendly faction label should not automatically prohibit lawful docking.

### Existing relationships to preserve

The public House index already supplies a useful web of interests:

- Aurelian and Emberhall cooperate through royal and industrial support.
- Aurelian relies on Marruk security despite rivalry and mistrust.
- Starweaver and Stormglass benefit from the connection between routes and shipbuilding.
- Starweaver and Blackwake have both convoy cooperation and tensions over control; these are compatible facets, not an automatic permanent war.
- Blackwake and Stormglass are identified as especially close to open conflict.
- Ironmantle materials support Emberhall manufacturing and Stormglass shipbuilding.
- Tidereach and Valecross cooperate where water, food, and Lifeveil systems overlap.
- Veyr financial leverage reaches many Houses, including security arrangements with Marruk.

**Proposed use:** Author contracts that engage these interests. Completing one job can create a defined obligation, opportunity, or complication with another party. Do not automatically apply symmetric approval/hostility changes to every allied or rival House.

## How this changes the Pillar 4 recommendations

1. **Faction work starts with integration.** Use the existing Houses, factions, guilds, and contacts rather than designing substitute organizations.
2. **Preserve multiple relationships.** A crew can be Freespacer, hold a Freebooter charter, work a Starweaver contract, and owe Veyr money. Those are different records with different consequences.
3. **Use identifiable opportunities and obligations.** A specific contract, contact, charter, license, or service entitlement is more actionable than an unexplained global favor number. Exact relationship states remain open.
4. **Connect rewards to supported consumers.** A route license must affect available travel; a blueprint commission must grant a real blueprint; a specialist introduction must reach recruitment; a service entitlement must be recognized by refit. Coordinate with EVT-03, COM-03, PRO-03, PRO-04, PRO-06, and PRO-07 in the [1.0 roadmap](ARKFLIGHT-1.0-ROADMAP.md).
5. **Use the existing political web for choices.** Escort, rescue, salvage, survey, containment, and prototype missions provide reasons to prepare different crews and equipment.
6. **Keep numerical rewards provisional.** The setting establishes identities and relationships, not automatic discounts, combat bonuses, loyalty scores, or guaranteed emergency aid. Those require explicit rules and balance work.

### Example mission connection — proposed, not established setting history

A Stormglass contact commissions recovery of a damaged prototype. The players select expedition support and equipment, travel to the wreck through an Event, and resolve any ship opposition through tactical Combat. The result can grant a real blueprint, produce salvage for fabrication, and create a specific follow-up obligation. A Blackwake competing claim could complicate the contract if explicitly authored. This connects existing pillars without replacing their separate rules.

## Approved starting selection and future additions

Approved for the 1.0 design scope on 16 October 2026:

| Starting group | Initial gameplay emphasis |
|---|---|
| Freespacers / Freebooters | Rescue, exploration, mutual aid, and earned Freebooter charters |
| Brotherhood of the Cosmic Flame | Imperial pressure, military opposition, rescue, and political complications |
| Grelkin Cartel | Raids, salvage, captured ships, clan contacts, and negotiation |
| House Starweaver and associated organizations | Routes, shipping contracts, and exploration |
| House Stormglass / Guild of Arkengineers | Upgrades, repairs, prototype work, recruitment, and training; retain separate organizational identities |
| Underwake Syndicates | Covert work, unusual equipment leads, contacts, and obligations |

This is enough setting material to begin playable integration. The user confirms additional factions exist; the catalog summarized here is not exhaustive. Retain all other recorded organizations for authored stories and contacts, and allow additional factions to be introduced later without rewriting the core systems.

The implementation must support extensible organization identities, relationships, contracts, and reward definitions, preserving custom entries and existing campaign data. A starting content focus does not remove the Council of the Void, Veilwardens, Faith of the Churn, remaining Houses, or civic organizations. Do not invent details for an unverified faction name.

Prioritize meaningful decisions, recognizable contacts, and usable rewards. Exact numerical perks, relationship states, and the initial number of missions remain open. Full approval and acceptance criteria for the eight review areas are in CRW-01 through CRW-08 of the roadmap.

## Existing Arkflight support and remaining integration work

- `src/generator/ayerstone-setting-catalog.js` already contains the principal faction labels and all ten House names.
- `src/generator/ayerstone-ship-doctrine.js` already assigns generation preferences to factions and Houses. Its exact weapon families, rooms, and mod preferences are Arkflight interpretations; the lore does not prescribe every loadout.
- `src/generator/ayerstone-crew-affiliation.js` already distinguishes House members from retainers. Its generation percentages are game implementation choices, not setting facts.
- `src/foundry/campaign-rewards.js` stores faction entries, route knowledge, and boons. The current review identified a need for gameplay consumers and clearer access to the ledger.
- `src/foundry/expedition-api.js` records faction/contact context as text. A future integration needs stable identities, relationship types, and source references without losing existing custom entries.
- Guild and civic organizations should have an appropriate service/contact representation. They do not all require selectable combat-generation doctrines.

This addition is documentation and source integration for the current review. Runtime faction benefits, recruitment rules, mission outcomes, and relationship mechanics remain to be designed and implemented.
